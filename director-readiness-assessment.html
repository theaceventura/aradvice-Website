Who This Is For

This assessment is designed for individual directors who want to know their personal oversight position — before anyone else tests it.

person

A director who has sat through a cyber or AI briefing, nodded along, and privately wondered whether the board was asking the right questions — or any questions at all.

gavel

A director whose organisation operates in a regulated sector, has experienced a cyber incident, or is preparing for a regulatory inquiry, and wants to understand their personal position before scrutiny arrives.

description

A director who sits on multiple boards and wants a documented, independent record of their own oversight readiness across every appointment.

What This Assessment Examines

This is not a technical audit. It is an independent assessment of what you are required to know and do as a director, and where your current practice sits against that standard.

What you are specifically expected to understand and do as a director across cyber risk oversight, AI governance, and IT general controls — mapped to Corporations Act s.180, ASIC guidance, the Cyber Security Act 2024, the Privacy Act, and (where relevant) APRA CPS 234.
