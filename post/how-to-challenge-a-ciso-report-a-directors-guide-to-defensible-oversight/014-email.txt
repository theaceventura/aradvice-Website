SUBJECT: CISO reports: Your liability when cyber oversight fails

When cyber incidents hit, ASIC and class action lawyers scrutinise how directors challenged CISO reports in board meetings. Under the Corporations Act, you're personally liable if your oversight appears passive or uninformed.
