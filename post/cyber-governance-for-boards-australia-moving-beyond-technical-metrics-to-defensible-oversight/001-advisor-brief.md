## How to respond — key messages

**Governance frameworks:** Andy should position his structured approach to cyber governance that translates technical controls into business risk language boards can actually govern. Emphasize frameworks that create clear accountability lines and defensible decision-making processes.

**Current reporting adequacy:** Most boards receive technical metrics they can't meaningfully interpret or act upon. Andy should offer a diagnostic of their current reporting against regulatory expectations.

**Better questions for CISOs:** Focus on risk appetite alignment, business impact scenarios, and control effectiveness rather than patch rates and vulnerability counts.

**Benchmarking:** Andy can position his cross-industry experience to show where their governance sits relative to peers and regulatory expectations.

**Breach preparedness:** Governance documentation and decision frameworks need to be in place before incidents occur. Post-breach is too late to establish defensible oversight.
