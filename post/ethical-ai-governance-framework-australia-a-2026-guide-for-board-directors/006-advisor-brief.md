## How to respond — key messages
• Defensible governance means documented processes, regular reporting, and evidence that the board actively challenged management assumptions about AI deployment and controls. It's about creating an audit trail that shows informed oversight, not rubber-stamping.
• Current adequacy depends on whether you have AI-specific risk assessment, clear accountability lines, and regular board reporting — most boards lack these basics. The exposure comes from failing to establish appropriate oversight mechanisms before issues emerge.
• Implement AI risk registers, require management to report against AICD guidance, and ensure technical advice explicitly references ACS professional standards. Document your challenge and oversight activities in board minutes.
