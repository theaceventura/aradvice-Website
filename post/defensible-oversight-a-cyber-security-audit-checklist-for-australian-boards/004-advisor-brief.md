## How to respond — key messages

**Audit adequacy**: Current technical audits may satisfy compliance boxes but won't demonstrate active board oversight that regulators expect under the business judgment rule. The framework shifts focus from "what's broken" to "how the board governs cyber risk."

**Defensible oversight definition**: Defensible means having documented evidence that the board actively questioned, challenged, and made informed decisions about cyber risk, not just received reports. It's about process and judgment, not just technical outcomes.

**Legal protection**: Following the checklist creates evidentiary foundation for business judgment rule protection but isn't a legal guarantee. The key is demonstrating informed, rational decision-making processes that courts and regulators will respect.

**Implementation practicality**: This requires retraining audit teams and CISOs to report on governance effectiveness, not just technical controls. Start with pilot sessions to reshape reporting before full implementation.
