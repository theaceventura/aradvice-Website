SUBJECT: Cyber Security Act 2024: your personal liability as a director

A board received a clean cybersecurity summary from management last quarter, voted to accept it, and six weeks later a ransomware incident exposed that the controls described in that paper had never been implemented. The director who signed off on that oversight now has no documented basis to demonstrate she applied the care and diligence required under Section 180 of the Corporations Act 2001, and ASIC's evidence-based expectations mean that reliance on management assurance alone is not a defence. The Cyber Security Act 2024 has made this a matter of personal accountability, not organisational risk.
