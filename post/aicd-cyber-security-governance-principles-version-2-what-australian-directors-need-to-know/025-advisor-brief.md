## How to respond -- key messages

**On dashboard quality and reporting**
A green dashboard is not evidence of safety, it is evidence that management controls what you see. Andy should recommend the board specify the reporting format it needs, including explicit coverage of legacy risk, event logging status, and open remediation items with funding status, rather than accepting whatever format management defaults to.

**On personal director liability under the Cyber Security Act 2024**
The Act creates mandatory reporting obligations that require board-level visibility of incident response in real time. Defensibility turns on documented evidence that the board was asking informed, specific questions before a crisis, not after it.

**On legacy IT and event logging**
These are the ASD-identified priority vulnerabilities for 2025 and 2026. The board question is not whether these risks exist but whether management has a funded, milestoned remediation plan and whether the board is tracking progress against it at each meeting.

**On AICD Version 2 changes**
The key additions are explicit treatment of digital supply chain risk and data governance. If the board has not reviewed its third-party risk oversight process since November 2024, that is a gap that needs to be addressed now.

**On building personal capability**
The standard is not technical expertise but informed inquiry. Directors need enough digital risk literacy to know which questions to ask and to recognise an inadequate answer when they hear one.
