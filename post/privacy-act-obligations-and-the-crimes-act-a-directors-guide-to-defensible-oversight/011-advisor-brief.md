## How to respond — key messages

Directors must establish documented oversight frameworks that include regular privacy reporting to the board, clear escalation procedures for incidents, and evidence of active engagement with privacy risks. The key is creating an audit trail that demonstrates informed decision-making rather than passive reliance on management assurances.

Criminal liability exposure arises when privacy breaches involve computer systems and data, particularly where directors can be shown to have been willfully blind or negligent in their oversight. Directors need incident response protocols that specifically address the intersection of privacy breaches and potential criminal activity.

The standard is defensible oversight, not perfect outcomes - directors who can demonstrate they established appropriate frameworks and actively engaged with privacy governance will be in a much stronger position than those who treated it as a compliance checkbox.
