## How to respond - key messages

**On current reporting adequacy:** Most boards are receiving activity metrics rather than effectiveness measures, which creates legal risk if gaps emerge during an incident. The board needs verification that controls match stated risk appetite.

**On independent verification:** This involves third-party assessment of maturity claims and establishment of verification processes that can withstand legal scrutiny. The cost is minimal compared to potential director liability exposure.

**On legal exposure:** Reliance on management assertions without independent verification constitutes governance failure under section 180, particularly in the current enforcement environment. Past acceptance of unverified reports creates documented risk.

**On interrogation techniques:** Boards should challenge maturity level claims, understand why specific target levels were chosen, and ensure alignment with data value and threat landscape. Focus on effectiveness rather than activity.

**On ransomware reporting:** The mandatory requirements create additional due diligence obligations and incident readiness expectations that require more rigorous governance processes.

**On defensible records:** Documentation must show active interrogation, independent verification, and board-level decisions on appropriate maturity targets based on risk appetite.
