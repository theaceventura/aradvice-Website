## How to respond — key messages

**On current reporting adequacy:** Most cyber dashboards focus on technical compliance metrics that create false confidence - you need reporting that shows actual resilience against realistic threat scenarios. Independent verification breaks the information asymmetry between technical teams and the board.

**On proportionate security:** This means capability matching the value of your information assets and realistic threat exposure, not just ticking compliance boxes. The regulatory environment in 2026 demands substance over form.

**On independent verification:** External assessment provides the defensive position you need if questioned by regulators, and ensures you see gaps before they become breaches. This should be regular, not one-off.

**On better questioning:** Focus on business impact scenarios rather than technical metrics - ask what happens when controls fail, not just whether they're in place.
