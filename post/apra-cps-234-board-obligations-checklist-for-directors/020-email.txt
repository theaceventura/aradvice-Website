SUBJECT: APRA CPS 234: Your board obligations checklist

Directors under APRA CPS 234 face personal accountability for cyber security oversight, yet many boards lack defensible processes to challenge management reports and demonstrate due diligence. You need specific frameworks to assess whether your organisation's cyber security reporting meets regulatory expectations and protects your position as a director. This checklist helps you identify gaps in board oversight and establish the questioning disciplines that demonstrate defensible governance.
