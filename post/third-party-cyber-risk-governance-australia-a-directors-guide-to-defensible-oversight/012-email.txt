SUBJECT: Third party cyber failures: Your personal director liability

Australian directors face personal liability when third-party vendors cause cyber incidents that breach ASIC and APRA requirements. Under current corporate law, claiming ignorance of vendor cyber risks is no longer a defensible position for directors.

This briefing outlines how to build oversight frameworks that satisfy regulatory scrutiny and protect you personally when vendor cyber failures occur. It covers the specific governance controls ASIC and APRA expect to see, plus practical board reporting structures that demonstrate defensible oversight.
