SUBJECT: AI Risks: Board oversight failures and director liability

The procurement committee approved a new AI system for customer service last month, but when the CEO asked about algorithmic bias monitoring at yesterday's board meeting, no director could explain what oversight was in place. Section 180 of the Corporations Act requires directors to exercise care and diligence, and courts are increasingly scrutinising whether boards have adequate processes to oversee AI-related risks. Personal liability follows when directors cannot demonstrate they took reasonable steps to understand and monitor the technology risks their company has assumed.
