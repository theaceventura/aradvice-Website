SUBJECT: Cyber Security Act 2024: Your personal liability as a director

The board approved the cyber risk report, the CISO presented the metrics, and everyone left the meeting satisfied they had done their job. If a regulator later asks what the board independently verified, that paper trail answers nothing. Under section 180 of the Corporations Act 2001, a director who relied entirely on management's technical framing without applying independent judgment has not discharged their duty of care, and personal liability follows from that failure, not corporate liability. In February 2026 the Federal Court imposed a $2.5 million penalty on FIIG Securities for inadequate cyber risk management, and the enforcement appetite that produced that outcome is now directed at boards.
