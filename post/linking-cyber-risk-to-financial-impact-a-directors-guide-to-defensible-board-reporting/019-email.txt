SUBJECT: Cyber risk reporting: Board liability for inadequate metrics

Directors face personal liability when cyber risk reports lack defensible financial quantification, especially under APRA CPS 234 and ASX governance principles. Most boards receive cyber briefings that focus on technical threats rather than quantified business impact, leaving directors unable to make informed decisions about risk appetite and investment priorities. This article provides a framework for linking cyber risks to specific financial metrics that satisfy regulatory expectations and support defensible board decision-making.
