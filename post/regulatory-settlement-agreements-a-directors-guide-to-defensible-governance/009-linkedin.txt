Your board's regulatory response plan likely assumes you'll fight any enforcement action in court. That assumption could cost you millions and destroy reputations unnecessarily.

Settlement agreements offer a controlled alternative to public litigation. They cap financial exposure, avoid admissions of liability, and keep remediation timelines realistic. Yet directors consistently underestimate their strategic value until they're already in ASIC's crosshairs.

The settlement process isn't surrender—it's sophisticated risk management. You negotiate penalty amounts, dispute factual allegations, and structure compliance programs on your terms rather than a court's timeline.

Smart boards prepare settlement frameworks before they need them. They identify negotiable elements, establish internal approval processes, and understand exactly which admissions they'll never make.

The question isn't whether you'll face regulatory scrutiny—it's whether you'll have the governance infrastructure to respond strategically when it arrives.
